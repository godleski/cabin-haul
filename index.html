    Parking: 5 cars, max
    2 in the garage, 3 in the driveway. The owner's car stays in the garage; leave it alone. No street parking anywhere in Lakota, they fine for it. Overflow lot is on the right as you turn into the neighborhood off US-40. Carpool if you can.

    House rules, the short list
    • No skis or boards inside the house. Garage only.
    • Trash goes in the bins in the garage. Pickup is Monday, so bag everything and get it out there before we leave Sunday.
    • Garage door closed when not in use.
    • Checkout is 10 AM Sunday. Hard stop.

    Getting around
    No Ubers in Winter Park. The Lift is a free shuttle that covers Winter Park and Fraser. The Lift Rider app shows where the bus is and when it gets to you.
